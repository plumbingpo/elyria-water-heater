How Westlake Homeowners Can Extend Water Heater Life

Routine maintenance makes all the difference between a water heater that runs for years and one that fails early. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: an annual sediment flush, an anode inspection every few years, a quick relief valve test once a year, a temperature setting around 120 degrees, and yearly descaling for any tankless unit on hard water, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

Equipment age plays a big role. With regular service, a tank water heater usually runs 8 to 12 years, a heat pump water heater 10 to 15, and a tankless unit up to 20. Once a heater passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like rust-colored water, popping noises, or a damp spot around the base, it's smart to start planning a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule is cheaper and causes far less stress than an emergency swap with water spreading across the floor.

The earlier you report a hot water problem, the cheaper it usually is. A sediment layer that's just starting to rumble needs only a flush, yet that same buildup, ignored through a few more years, can overheat the bottom of the tank and burn out the lower element on an electric model, turning a simple visit into a replacement. Save our number in your contacts now so help is one call away the moment something sounds or looks wrong. Early intervention saves time, money, and cold showers.

Typical Water Heater Issues in Westlake

In Westlake, we work on every kind of water heater, and each one has a typical way of failing. Gas tanks tend to have trouble with pilot assemblies and thermocouples, sooty burners, and venting that doesn't draft. Electric tanks burn out elements and thermostats, particularly when sediment buries the lower element. Tankless heaters show error codes for scale buildup, ignition faults, and flow sensor trouble, most of which trace back to skipped descaling. Heat pump water heaters need clean air filters and a clear condensate drain, and they don't do well in small, closed-in spaces. We service all four types regularly and stock the common parts to finish most repairs in one trip.
