Restaurant Services

Food-Service Hot Water Systems Built for the Rush

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest places to keep supplied with hot water. Prep sinks, pre-rinse sprayers, and dish cycles all draw from the same tank, a lunch rush can empty a small heater in minutes, and the dish machine depends on a booster to reach its final rinse temperature. Elyria Water Heater services, sizes, and repairs the tanks, tankless units, and booster heaters that keep both the kitchen and the restrooms supplied, and we schedule visits around your operating hours.

We verify temperatures at the tank, the booster, and the fixtures and document every visit in written reports your inspector and landlord can review.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians are used to working in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, keeping downtime short, and staging temporary hot water whenever the layout allows.

Safe Installation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can result in carbon monoxide from a blocked or undersized vent, scalding at the tap, bacterial growth in tanks kept too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Elyria Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas piping sizing, relief valve and discharge line routing, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where required, and permits and inspections. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test dates, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms.
